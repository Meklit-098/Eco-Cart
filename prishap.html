Water Bottles

Biodegradable water bottles are made from natural materials like cornstarch or sugarcane that breaks down easily in our enviorment. They help reduce plastic pollution and protect wildlife from harmful waste. Plastic water bottles cause problems because when people throw them away, they take long to break down and can harm animals.
