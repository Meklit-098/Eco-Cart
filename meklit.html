Phone Cases

Phone cases are an item most people with phones have to keep their phones protected. Not only is it protective, but it is also stylish. Because of this, people replace their phone cases more often. According to Podbase, "Two in five Americans replace phone accessories every six months." With the mass replacement of phone cases comes an increase in trash and waste in the environment. However, if phone cases are made with more eco-friendly materials, this issue can be prevented.

All of the products linked below are from the brand AGood, a phone case brand that focuses on creating eco-friendly phone cases with biodegradable materials.
